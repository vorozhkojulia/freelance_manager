# Surface brief: Freelance Manager app shell (Operate, redesigned)

## Direction contract
THESIS: A designer's studio wall. Pinned projects are colored sticky notes you can drag, tick and unpin; charts are notes and bars that light up together. Refused: strict ticket-rail/SaaS-card tidiness. Brief-pinned by the user, so the roll did not decide it.
OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo felt with a dot texture; six sticky colors (lemon, pink, mint, sky, peach, lilac) that follow each project across every screen; paper sheets for charts and lists; Kalam handwriting for project names and the Next-up line, Archivo for UI, JetBrains Mono for figures; orange pushpins and signal orange for urgency only.
STORY: Open it and the wall shows what is next, what is late, where the week overflows, and how much money is where.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Date and the handwritten Next-up line top-left, Tidy up and New project top-right, overload banner, then the row of pinned sticky notes tilted a little, with the six-week runway chart and receipt beneath.
FORM: Studio pinboard, candidate 1 of 7 in the user-pinned redesign; seed afe183e1 rolled but overridden by the brief.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
